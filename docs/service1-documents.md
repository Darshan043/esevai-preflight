# Service 1 – Community Certificate (Tamil Nadu e-Sevai)

- Department: Revenue Administration
- Service: Community Certificate
- Service code: REV-101

The service code REV-101 is taken from the official FAQ, which tells the citizen to open "REV-101 Community Certificate". The Service List row for this service names the department as Revenue Administration and the service as Community Certificate. That row does not print the code.

Sources checked: 16 November 2026

**Source 1 – Official Tamil Nadu e-Sevai FAQ, Question 7** ("What are the documents required for applying Community Certificate?"):

https://www.tnesevai.tn.gov.in/citizen/Pages/FAQ.aspx#collapseSeven

This page was fetched on 16 November 2026. It lists four items:

1. Photo
2. Address Proof
3. Community Certificate of Father or Community Certificate of Mother or Community Certificate of Siblings
4. Self-Declaration of Applicant

**Source 2 – Official Tamil Nadu e-Sevai Service List:**

https://www.tnesevai.tn.gov.in/citizen/ServiceList.aspx

That exact URL returned HTTP 404 on 16 November 2026. The Community Certificate row was read the same day from the live Service List page:

https://www.tnesevai.tn.gov.in/citizen/Pages/ServiceList.aspx

That row lists five items:

1. Photo
2. Any Address Proof
3. Community Certificate of Father or Community Certificate of Mother or Community Certificate of Siblings
4. Self-Declaration of Applicant
5. DNC Certificate

## Unresolved discrepancy

The FAQ lists four items. The Service List lists those four and additionally lists DNC Certificate. This difference is not resolved. DNC Certificate still needs confirmation against the current Community Certificate application workflow before it is treated as required.

The FAQ says "Address Proof". The Service List says "Any Address Proof". Neither source gives accepted formats, a Mandatory/Optional label, or a validity rule.
